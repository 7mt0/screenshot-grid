Selected frame

Drag the bottom edge of a frame to make its row shorter or taller. Drag inside a strip or zoomed frame (or scroll on a strip) to choose what shows. To swap two frames, drag one by its ⠿ handle in the corner onto the other. Drop a screenshot file on a frame to replace it. Scroll on a full frame, or Ctrl+scroll on a strip, to zoom it.
