Format

X uploads images up to 5 MB and 4096 × 4096 px. Keep under both and it won't shrink your grid further.

Your screenshots stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Click a frame in the grid to adjust its zoom, position and row height.

X Y

Drag the bottom edge of a frame to make its row shorter or taller. Drag inside a strip or zoomed frame to choose what shows. To swap two frames, drag one by its ⠿ handle in the corner onto the other. Drop a screenshot file on a frame to replace it. Scroll on any frame to zoom it. While dragging, the picture snaps to the centre and a red line shows it — hold Alt to move freely. Arrow keys nudge it 1 px (Shift+arrows: 10 px).

Add your screenshots

Pick as many caps as you like, drop them here, or paste with Ctrl+V. The tool suggests every grid that fits them.
